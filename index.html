Sports Comparatives

Which sport would you choose for Sports Day?

Your task

Choose two sports. Write a short paragraph comparing them and recommend one for your class.

  1. Write three sentences comparing the two sports. Use a different adjective in each sentence.
  2. Use at least one comparative with -er than and one with more … than.
  3. Finish by saying which sport you recommend and why. Use because.

Example

Basketball is faster than volleyball. Volleyball is easier to set up in our gym than basketball. I think basketball is more exciting than volleyball. I recommend basketball because we can make teams and play a short tournament.
